Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 (v1.0.2)

SmartGoCharge ("the app") is developed by dBR Promotions. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

SmartGoCharge is an unofficial companion app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected to EDF Energy or EDF SA.

1. Data We Collect

SmartGoCharge stores only the minimum data required to operate:

  • Your EDF Energy account credentials (email address and password) — used solely to authenticate with the EDF Energy API
  • Your EDF account number and associated meter/tariff details — retrieved from the EDF API and cached locally
  • Smart device information (vehicle make/model, charger make/model) — retrieved from the EDF API and cached locally
  • A JWT authentication token — issued by EDF Energy upon login and stored temporarily for API access
  • Smart charging session history (completed sessions) — retrieved from the EDF API and stored on your device so your history is preserved beyond the EDF API’s 12-hour retrieval window

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device only. SmartGoCharge does not operate any servers and does not transmit your data to any infrastructure controlled by the app developer.

  • Your EDF account credentials and API token are stored in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the operating system with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection
  • Account, tariff, and device information is cached in the app's local UserDefaults storage on your device
  • No data is written to iCloud, third-party analytics services, or any remote storage

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used solely to:

  • Authenticate with the EDF Energy Kraken API on your behalf
  • Display your smart charging schedule, preferences, and tariff information
  • Send charging control commands (boost, suspend/resume, set preferences) to your registered smart device via the EDF API

SmartGoCharge does not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond the above.

4. Third-Party API Access

SmartGoCharge communicates directly with the following third-party services operated by EDF Energy:

  • Kraken GraphQL API — for account data, device preferences, and smart charging control. All requests are authenticated using the token issued at login.
  • EDF Products REST API — for electricity unit rate and standing charge data. This endpoint is publicly accessible and no personal data is sent.

Use of these APIs is subject to EDF Energy's own terms of service and privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing

SmartGoCharge does not share your data with any third party beyond the EDF Energy APIs described above. Your credentials and account data never leave your device except as part of authenticated API requests made directly to EDF Energy's servers.

6. Data Deletion

You can remove all locally stored data at any time:

  • Account → Delete App Data — permanently removes all credentials, cached account data, and charging history from your device. Your EDF Energy account is not affected.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all data stored by the app

To delete your EDF Energy account itself, please contact EDF Energy directly at edfenergy.com/contact. SmartGoCharge does not create or manage EDF accounts.

7. Data Accuracy

All data displayed by SmartGoCharge — including charging sessions, tariff rates, and estimated costs — is sourced directly from EDF Energy's APIs at the time of the last refresh. This data is presented as-is and may not always reflect the most current state of your account.

  • Estimated costs are calculated using tariff rate information provided by EDF Energy and are intended as a guide only. Actual charges on your bill may differ.
  • Charging session history is limited to data made available by the EDF API and stored on your device. Sessions not captured within the API's retrieval window may not appear.
  • Always refer to your official EDF Energy bill or online account for accurate and definitive billing information.

The developer of SmartGoCharge accepts no responsibility for discrepancies between information shown in the app and data held by EDF Energy.

8. Security

Your credentials are stored using the iOS Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection, meaning they cannot be accessed when the device is locked and are not included in iCloud backups.

9. Children's Privacy

SmartGoCharge is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
